Major World Currencies

The Euro (€) is the official currency of the Eurozone. The Japanese Yen remains one of the most traded currencies worldwide.

The Chinese Yuan (Renminbi) has gained significant international importance. Many people confuse the Indian Rupee (INR) with its subdivision, the Paise.
The Swiss Franc (CHF) is considered one of the most stable currencies in the world. - Financial Times Editorial
